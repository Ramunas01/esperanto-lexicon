The scripts (src/analyzer/review/)

- review_context.py <lemma> — one call assembles everything: sense-diverse corpus sentences + match count, the Esperanto anchor round-trip (does the eo_word's ESPDIC gloss actually mean the lemma?), alternatives with their glosses, a name signal, and a one-line ACCEPT / REJECT(name) / ASK recommendation.
- review_record.py — logs decisions (idempotent, last-write-wins), with optional anchor override.
- review_merge.py — folds decisions into a fresh view-only gapfill_review.reviewed.xlsx.
- set_completeness.py — closed-set audit.
- export_snapshot.py — Part D setup; snapshots + seeds your prior decisions, and refuses to run if Excel has the file open.
